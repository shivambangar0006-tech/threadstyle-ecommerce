# ThreadStyle – Week 1 Project Plan

## Project Overview
ThreadStyle is an apparel and fashion e-commerce website concept designed to provide customers with a simple way to discover clothing products, view product information, and manage items in a shopping cart.

## Target Users
Students, young adults, and general online shoppers looking for an affordable and easy-to-browse apparel shopping experience.

## Planned Features
- Home page
- Product catalogue and categories
- Search and filtering
- Product details
- Shopping cart
- Responsive mobile and desktop layout
- Backend/API integration
- Testing and performance evaluation

## Four-Week Roadmap
Week 1 – Research, requirements, planning, competitor considerations, and risk analysis.
Week 2 – UI/UX design and responsive front-end prototype.
Week 3 – Backend architecture, database planning, and API integration.
Week 4 – Functional testing, responsiveness testing, performance review, and final improvements.

## Risk Management
Security risks will be reduced through input validation and careful handling of data. Performance risks will be addressed through optimized assets and efficient code. Usability risks will be reduced through responsive design and clear navigation. Development delays will be managed with weekly milestones.

## Expected Outcome
The final project will demonstrate an organized apparel e-commerce website concept with a responsive interface, planned backend/API functionality, and documented testing.
